06 / The way forward

Better journeys
are possible.

There is no single solution to every transport problem. Improvements should respond to the needs, geography, and resources of each community.

↗

Plan routes around people.

Use commuter demand and community feedback to identify underserved areas, important destinations, and difficult transfer points.

₱

Keep transport affordable.

Review fare burdens, improve connections between services, and make fare information clear so commuters can plan their expenses.

◷

Consider real operating hours.

Study when people actually travel, including early mornings, evenings, weekends, and shift-work schedules.

✳

Make stops safer and accessible.

Improve lighting, shelters, sidewalks, crossings, seating, ramps, and clear signs at transport waiting areas.

≋

Share reliable route information.

Provide easy-to-understand route maps, fare guides, operating hours, and service announcements through accessible channels.

◎

Include commuters in decisions.

Consult students, workers, older adults, persons with disabilities, drivers, and local communities before planning changes.
