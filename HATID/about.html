01 / About HATID

Mobility is more
than movement.

It is the ability to reach school, work, healthcare, markets, family, and opportunities—without unnecessary barriers.

“Getting home should not be a privilege. It should be part of a transport system that works for everyone.”

Why HATID exists

For many Filipinos, commuting is part of everyday life. Yet a journey can become difficult when routes are limited, fares take up too much of a household budget, vehicles are unavailable, or waiting areas feel unsafe.

These challenges can be especially visible in provincial communities and smaller cities, where travel options and operating hours may be more limited than in major urban centers.

Our mission

To raise awareness about transportation barriers experienced by Filipino commuters and encourage practical, inclusive solutions that make everyday travel more accessible, affordable, available, safe, and reliable.

Our vision

A Philippines where people can travel with dignity and confidence, regardless of their income, age, ability, location, or schedule.

01 / ACCESSIBILITY

Reachable by everyone.

Transport should be usable by people of different ages and abilities, including persons with disabilities, older adults, and people carrying goods or traveling with children.

02 / AFFORDABILITY

Fair costs for every trip.

Transportation expenses should not prevent people from attending school, going to work, or accessing essential services.

03 / AVAILABILITY

There when people need it.

Routes, vehicles, and operating hours should reflect the real schedules and travel needs of communities.

04 / SAFETY & RELIABILITY

Travel with confidence.

Commuters need safe waiting spaces, clear information, dependable service, and infrastructure that protects everyone.

HATID.

A Journey Within Reach. Advocating for accessible, affordable, available, safe, and reliable transportation for every Filipino.
