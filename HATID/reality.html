01 / The cost of mobility

Transport is a significant part of household expenditure.

PSA National Accounts show that household final consumption expenditure on transport increased from ₱1.52 trillion in 2022 to ₱1.98 trillion in 2024, measured at current prices. This is a national expenditure total—not the average amount spent by one household, and not public-transport fares alone.

The figures are at current prices, so changes reflect both quantities and price changes. They should not be interpreted as a pure increase in the amount of transport consumed.

02 / Road safety

Safe journeys are part of accessible transportation.

The PSA reported a national road-traffic-injury mortality rate of 12.2 deaths per 100,000 population in 2024. Rates varied across regions: Ilocos Region recorded 20.0, Cagayan Valley 26.1, and Central Luzon 12.5 per 100,000.

These are mortality rates, not counts of all crashes or injuries. They show why safe roads, crossings, public spaces, and transport planning should be part of the conversation.

What these numbers tell us: National spending data show that transport is a substantial household consumption category. Road-safety data show that risks differ across regions. Together, they support HATID's call for transport systems that are affordable, accessible, safe, and reliable. They do not establish how often commuters in a particular town cannot find a ride after 6 PM.
